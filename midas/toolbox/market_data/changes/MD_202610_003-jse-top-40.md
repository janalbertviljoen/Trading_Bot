## Description

The user may hold value picks of JSE-listed companies (the TFSA cannot hold single
shares, so in the EasyEquities ZAR account), and wants the picks compared with the
FTSE/JSE Top 40. The value tool reads US data only: SEC EDGAR fundamentals, Yahoo
prices, the S&P 500 membership from Wikipedia.

## Assessment (2026-10-31)

The effort splits in two, very unequal parts.

- **Prices, dividends and the Top 40 benchmark: small.** Yahoo Finance serves JSE
  shares (`.JO` symbols) in ZAR through the same source code (`sources/prices.py`); a
  total-return benchmark can be a Top 40 ETF's adjusted close, as the sector funds are
  for the S&P 500. Current Top 40 members are public; their history is harder (FTSE
  Russell publishes changes, not as a free table). A returns-only comparison of the
  picks with the Top 40, in ZAR via `DEXSFUS`, is about a day's work.
- **Fundamentals for the value rules: large.** There is no JSE equivalent of EDGAR's
  XBRL company facts: annual reports are PDFs, and no free source gives first-reported,
  dated figures. Yahoo's fundamentals are restated and short, which the quant rules
  forbid for scoring (`midas/instructions/quant.md`). Scoring JSE companies needs a
  paid point-in-time fundamentals vendor (to be researched: coverage, price,
  history), which reopens the data-budget decision of spec §9.1.

## Open decisions (user)

- Returns-only Top 40 comparison first, or wait until a fundamentals source is chosen?
- Budget for a paid JSE fundamentals source?
