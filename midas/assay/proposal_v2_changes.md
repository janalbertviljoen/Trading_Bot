## Calculations checked

- Plus contribution: $4 × 12 × (1 − 0.15) − $8 = $32.80 (v1 said about $33).
- Contribution at 1,500 / 2,000 payers: $49,200 / $65,600, before fixed costs.
- Downloads at 2.2%: 1,500 → 68,182; 2,000 → 90,909 (v1: 75,000–100,000 sign-ups, at
  2%).
- Premium contribution: $15 × 12 × 0.85 − $8 = $145.
- Scenarios (conservative, base, upside), with the free users' chat cost: 2,364 / 1,715
  / 1,200 Plus subscribers; revenue $113,472 / $86,820 / $75,600; operating profit
  $50,016 / $50,009 / $50,019. The spreadsheet formulas and a Python check agree.
- Free users' chat cost per payer: $0.10 × (1/0.015 − 1) = $6.57; $0.05 × (1/0.022 − 1)
  = $2.22; $0.02 × (1/0.03 − 1) = $0.65.
- Commission sensitivity: 10% of Plus net revenue = $4.08 per Plus subscriber a year.
